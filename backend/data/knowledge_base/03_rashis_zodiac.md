# The Twelve Rashis (Zodiac Signs) in Jyotish

The 360-degree zodiac belt is divided into twelve 30-degree segments termed Rashis. In Vedic sidereal astrology, signs are tied to fixed stellar constellations using an Ayanamsha (e.g. Lahiri).

## 1. Mesha (Aries) - 0° to 30°
- Element: Fire (Agni) | Quality: Movable (Chara) | Ruling Planet: Mangala (Mars)
- Characteristics: Dynamic, pioneer, bold, direct, impulsive, high physical vitality.

## 2. Vrishabha (Taurus) - 30° to 60°
- Element: Earth (Prithvi) | Quality: Fixed (Sthira) | Ruling Planet: Shukra (Venus)
- Characteristics: Stable, sensory appreciation, endurance, practical values, artistic flair.

## 3. Mithuna (Gemini) - 60° to 90°
- Element: Air (Vayu) | Quality: Dual (Dwisvabhava) | Ruling Planet: Budha (Mercury)
- Characteristics: Curious, intellectual, communicative, adaptable, versatile, social.

## 4. Karka (Cancer) - 90° to 120°
- Element: Water (Jala) | Quality: Movable (Chara) | Ruling Planet: Chandra (Moon)
- Characteristics: Nurturing, empathetic, protective, emotionally perceptive, deeply loyal.

## 5. Simha (Leo) - 120° to 150°
- Element: Fire (Agni) | Quality: Fixed (Sthira) | Ruling Planet: Surya (Sun)
- Characteristics: Noble, generous, charismatic, dignified, authoritative, dramatic.

## 6. Kanya (Virgo) - 150° to 180°
- Element: Earth (Prithvi) | Quality: Dual (Dwisvabhava) | Ruling Planet: Budha (Mercury)
- Characteristics: Analytical, meticulous, methodical, service-oriented, perfectionist.

## 7. Tula (Libra) - 180° to 210°
- Element: Air (Vayu) | Quality: Movable (Chara) | Ruling Planet: Shukra (Venus)
- Characteristics: Harmonious, diplomatic, aesthetic, balanced, partnership-focused.

## 8. Vrishchika (Scorpio) - 210° to 240°
- Element: Water (Jala) | Quality: Fixed (Sthira) | Ruling Planet: Mangala (Mars) / Ketu
- Characteristics: Intense, penetrative, resolute, private, transformative, investigative.

## 9. Dhanu (Sagittarius) - 240° to 270°
- Element: Fire (Agni) | Quality: Dual (Dwisvabhava) | Ruling Planet: Guru (Jupiter)
- Characteristics: Philosophical, optimistic, quest for truth, freedom-loving, ethical.

## 10. Makara (Capricorn) - 270° to 300°
- Element: Earth (Prithvi) | Quality: Movable (Chara) | Ruling Planet: Shani (Saturn)
- Characteristics: Pragmatic, persevering, structured, ambitious, disciplined.

## 11. Kumbha (Aquarius) - 300° to 330°
- Element: Air (Vayu) | Quality: Fixed (Sthira) | Ruling Planet: Shani (Saturn) / Rahu
- Characteristics: Visionary, humanitarian, egalitarian, unconventional, intellectual.

## 12. Meena (Pisces) - 330° to 360°
- Element: Water (Jala) | Quality: Dual (Dwisvabhava) | Ruling Planet: Guru (Jupiter)
- Characteristics: Compassionate, imaginative, contemplative, spiritual, boundless intuition.

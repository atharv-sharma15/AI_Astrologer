# The 27 Nakshatras (Lunar Mansions) in Vedic Astrology

In Vedic astrology, the ecliptic is divided into 27 equal segments of 13°20' (13 degrees 20 minutes) called Nakshatras. Each Nakshatra is further subdivided into 4 quarters called Padas (3°20' each), linking the 27 Nakshatras precisely to the 12 Signs (108 Padas total).

## Nakshatras and Planetary Lords (Vimshottari Scheme)

1. **Ashwini** (0°00' - 13°20' Aries) | Deity: Ashwini Kumaras | Lord: Ketu | Symbol: Horse's Head
   - Qualities: Swift, healing, initiative, pioneer energy.
2. **Bharani** (13°20' - 26°40' Aries) | Deity: Yama | Lord: Venus | Symbol: Yoni
   - Qualities: Transformation, endurance, bearing burdens, creativity.
3. **Krittika** (26°40' Aries - 10°00' Taurus) | Deity: Agni | Lord: Sun | Symbol: Razor / Flame
   - Qualities: Sharp determination, purification, digestion, candor.
4. **Rohini** (10°00' - 23°20' Taurus) | Deity: Brahma | Lord: Moon | Symbol: Cart / Chariot
   - Qualities: Growth, beauty, fertility, agriculture, artistic charm.
5. **Mrigashira** (23°20' Taurus - 6°40' Gemini) | Deity: Soma | Lord: Mars | Symbol: Deer's Head
   - Qualities: Searching, curiosity, gentle nature, quest for fulfillment.
6. **Ardra** (6°40' - 20°00' Gemini) | Deity: Rudra | Lord: Rahu | Symbol: Teardrop
   - Qualities: Catharsis, overcoming storms, technological insight, emotional clearing.
7. **Punarvasu** (20°00' Gemini - 3°20' Cancer) | Deity: Aditi | Lord: Jupiter | Symbol: Bow and Quiver
   - Qualities: Return of light, renewal, restoration, optimism, benevolence.
8. **Pushya** (3°20' - 16°40' Cancer) | Deity: Brihaspati | Lord: Saturn | Symbol: Cow's Udder / Flower
   - Qualities: Considered the most nourishing and auspicious star; spiritual nourishment, wisdom.
9. **Ashlesha** (16°40' - 30°00' Cancer) | Deity: Nagas | Lord: Mercury | Symbol: Coiled Serpent
   - Qualities: Psychological depth, intuition, mystical perception, shrewd insight.
10. **Magha** (0°00' - 13°20' Leo) | Deity: Pitris (Ancestors) | Lord: Ketu | Symbol: Royal Throne
    - Qualities: Lineage, ancestral heritage, honor, dignity, leadership.
11. **Purva Phalguni** (13°20' - 26°40' Leo) | Deity: Bhaga | Lord: Venus | Symbol: Hammock / Couch
    - Qualities: Leisure, creative manifestation, affection, relaxed prosperity.
12. **Uttara Phalguni** (26°40' Leo - 10°00' Virgo) | Deity: Aryaman | Lord: Sun | Symbol: Bed / Sun Bed
    - Qualities: Patronage, contractual relationships, philanthropy, dependable loyalty.
13. **Hasta** (10°00' - 23°20' Virgo) | Deity: Savitur | Lord: Moon | Symbol: Open Hand
    - Qualities: Craftsmanship, dexterity, healing hands, detail orientation.
14. **Chitra** (23°20' Virgo - 6°40' Libra) | Deity: Tvashtar | Lord: Mars | Symbol: Brilliant Jewel
    - Qualities: Architectural design, aesthetics, visual brilliance, craftsmanship.
15. **Swati** (6°40' - 20°00' Libra) | Deity: Vayu | Lord: Rahu | Symbol: Young Sprout
    - Qualities: Independence, adaptability, commerce, gentle breeze, freedom.
16. **Vishakha** (20°00' Libra - 3°20' Scorpio) | Deity: Indra-Agni | Lord: Jupiter | Symbol: Triumphal Arch
    - Qualities: Singular focus, triumph, goal achievement, passionate determination.
17. **Anuradha** (3°20' - 16°40' Scorpio) | Deity: Mitra | Lord: Saturn | Symbol: Lotus / Staff
    - Qualities: Devotion, organizational diplomacy, friendship, resilience under pressure.
18. **Jyeshtha** (16°40' - 30°00' Scorpio) | Deity: Indra | Lord: Mercury | Symbol: Amulet / Umbrella
    - Qualities: Seniority, protective instincts, mental acumen, defense of truth.
19. **Mula** (0°00' - 13°20' Sagittarius) | Deity: Nirriti | Lord: Ketu | Symbol: Tied Roots
    - Qualities: Investigating root causes, radical honesty, dismantling illusion.
20. **Purva Ashadha** (13°20' - 26°40' Sagittarius) | Deity: Apas (Waters) | Lord: Venus | Symbol: Elephant Tusk / Fan
    - Qualities: Invincibility, emotional purification, deep conviction.
21. **Uttara Ashadha** (26°40' Sagittarius - 10°00' Capricorn) | Deity: Vishvadevas | Lord: Sun | Symbol: Elephant Tusk
    - Qualities: Universal harmony, unshakeable integrity, enduring achievement.
22. **Shravana** (10°00' - 23°20' Capricorn) | Deity: Vishnu | Lord: Moon | Symbol: Three Footprints / Ear
    - Qualities: Deep listening, oral learning, scholarly study, preservation of dharma.
23. **Dhanishta** (23°20' Capricorn - 6°40' Aquarius) | Deity: Eight Vasus | Lord: Mars | Symbol: Drum (Mridangam)
    - Qualities: Rhythm, musicality, material wealth, synchronization.
24. **Shatabhisha** (6°40' - 20°00' Aquarius) | Deity: Varuna | Lord: Rahu | Symbol: Empty Circle / 100 Physicians
    - Qualities: Healing sciences, containment, seclusion, enigmatic discernment.
25. **Purva Bhadrapada** (20°00' Aquarius - 3°20' Pisces) | Deity: Aja Ekapada | Lord: Jupiter | Symbol: Front of Funeral Cot
    - Qualities: Spiritual intensity, ascetic resolve, philosophical detachment.
26. **Uttara Bhadrapada** (3°20' - 16°40' Pisces) | Deity: Ahirbudhnya | Lord: Saturn | Symbol: Back of Funeral Cot
    - Qualities: Serenity, deep ocean contemplation, patient wisdom, inner peace.
27. **Revati** (16°40' - 30°00' Pisces) | Deity: Pushan | Lord: Mercury | Symbol: Fish / Drum
    - Qualities: Final completion, guidance of travelers, gentle nurturing, spiritual culmination.

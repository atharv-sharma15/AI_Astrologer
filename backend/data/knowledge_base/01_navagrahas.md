# The Navagrahas: The Nine Astrological Forces in Jyotish

In classical Vedic astrology (Jyotish), the celestial bodies and mathematical intersection points are known as Grahas—literally "that which grasps or influences." There are nine primary Grahas (Navagrahas) studied in Parashari astrology.

## 1. Surya (The Sun)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Soul (Atman), father, vitality, self-confidence, leadership, authority, dignity, and bone structure.
- **Ruler of:** Simha (Leo).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Mesha (Aries) at 10 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Tula (Libra) at 10 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Mild natural malefic (Krura) due to intense heat, but pure in essence (Sattvic).

## 2. Chandra (The Moon)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Mind (Manas), emotions, mother, nourishment, public interaction, intuition, memory, and bodily fluids.
- **Ruler of:** Karka (Cancer).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Vrishabha (Taurus) at 3 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Vrishchika (Scorpio) at 3 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Natural benefic when bright (Shukla Paksha), mild malefic when dark (Krishna Paksha near Amavasya).

## 3. Mangala (Mars)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Energy, initiative, physical courage, younger siblings, property, technical skill, logic, surgery, defense.
- **Ruler of:** Mesha (Aries) and Vrishchika (Scorpio).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Makara (Capricorn) at 28 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Karka (Cancer) at 28 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Natural malefic (Papagraha).

## 4. Budha (Mercury)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Intellect (Buddhi), speech, analytical reasoning, communication, writing, trade, humor, adaptability.
- **Ruler of:** Mithuna (Gemini) and Kanya (Virgo).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Kanya (Virgo) at 15 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Meena (Pisces) at 15 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Naturally benefic, but mutable; adopts the nature of planets it conjuncts.

## 5. Guru / Brihaspati (Jupiter)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Wisdom, higher knowledge, guru/teachers, children, prosperity, dharma, optimism, spirituality, morality.
- **Ruler of:** Dhanu (Sagittarius) and Meena (Pisces).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Karka (Cancer) at 5 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Makara (Capricorn) at 5 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Greatest natural benefic (Subhagraha).

## 6. Shukra (Venus)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Love, devotion, marital relationships, artistic talent, aesthetics, beauty, luxury, refined diplomacy.
- **Ruler of:** Vrishabha (Taurus) and Tula (Libra).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Meena (Pisces) at 27 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Kanya (Virgo) at 27 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Natural benefic (Subhagraha).

## 7. Shani (Saturn)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Discipline, endurance, duty, delay, patience, humility, longevity, service, chronic lessons, time (Kala).
- **Ruler of:** Makara (Capricorn) and Kumbha (Aquarius).
- **Exaltation (Uccha):** Tula (Libra) at 20 degrees.
- **Debilitation (Neecha):** Mesha (Aries) at 20 degrees.
- **Natural Benefic/Malefic:** Natural malefic (Papagraha), teachings through perseverance.

## 8. Rahu (North Node of the Moon)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Unconventional ambition, worldly fascination, foreign lands, technology, innovation, obsession, maya (illusion).
- **Nature:** Shadow planet (Chhaya Graha) without physical mass. Amplifies the ruler of the sign it occupies.

## 9. Ketu (South Node of the Moon)
- **Significations (Karakas):** Detachment, liberation (Moksha), spiritual insights, ascetics, unexpected insights, past-life patterns.
- **Nature:** Shadow planet (Chhaya Graha). Always situated 180 degrees opposite Rahu.

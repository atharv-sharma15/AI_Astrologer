# Core Principles of Vedic Chart Interpretation

Vedic astrology (Jyotish) is guided by holistic interpretive rules derived from foundational treatises such as Maharishi Parashara's *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*.

## 1. The Primacy of the Lagna (Ascendant)
The Lagna and its ruling planet (Lagnesha) represent the root of the tree of life. If the Lagna is strong (placed in Kendra or Trikona, aspected by natural benefics like Jupiter or Venus), it provides vitality and resilience to withstand difficult transits or Dasha periods.

## 2. Retrograde Planets (Vakri Grahas)
- A retrograde planet appears to move backwards from the geocentric viewpoint because of relative orbital speeds with Earth.
- In Vedic astrology, retrograde motion imparts Chesta Bala (motional strength). A retrograde planet is closer to Earth and exhibits intensified, introspective energy.
- Retrograde natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus) stimulate deep inner wisdom and unconventional blessings.
- Retrograde natural malefics (Saturn, Mars) demand careful patience, unhurried decisions, and resolving past karmic lessons.

## 3. Combustion (Asta)
- When a planet comes within close angular proximity to Surya (The Sun), it is termed combust (Asta).
- The physical significations governed by that planet may face temporary suppression or require extra effort, whereas spiritual and internal significations often become purified and intensely focused.

## 4. Planetary Relationships (Sambandha) and Aspects (Drishti)
- All planets cast a direct 7th house aspect (full sight).
- Special aspects in Parashari system:
  - **Mars:** Aspects 4th, 7th, and 8th houses from its placement.
  - **Jupiter:** Aspects 5th, 7th, and 9th houses (the trine aspects of blessing).
  - **Saturn:** Aspects 3rd, 7th, and 10th houses (aspects of labor, perseverance, and karma).
  - **Rahu & Ketu:** Often considered to cast 5th and 9th aspects similar to Jupiter.

## 5. Dasha Systems (Planetary Timing)
The Vimshottari Dasha is the most widely utilized timing cycle, spanning 120 years divided unevenly among the nine Grahas:
- Ketu (7 years), Venus (20 years), Sun (6 years), Moon (10 years), Mars (7 years), Rahu (18 years), Jupiter (16 years), Saturn (19 years), Mercury (17 years).
- The Mahadasha (major cycle) and Antardasha (sub-cycle) bring the latent themes of the ruling planet into active lived experience.

## 6. Ethical Demarcation & Humility
In traditional Jyotish:
- Astrology reveals tendencies (Pravritti), not rigid fatalism. Human free will (Purushartha) and spiritual practices (Sadhana, prayer, conscious righteous action) are essential factors.
- Predictions must never be used to induce fear or fatalistic dependency.

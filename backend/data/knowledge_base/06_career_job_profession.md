# Career, Job, and Profession in Vedic Astrology (करियर, आजीविका एवं नौकरी)

In Vedic astrology (Jyotish), livelihood and professional destiny are analyzed primarily through the 10th House (कर्म भाव - Karma Bhava), the 6th House (सेवा भाव - Service and Employment), and their governing planetary archetypes.

## 1. The 10th House (कर्म भाव - Karma Bhava)
- **Significance:** Social status, career prestige, leadership, executive authority, government recognition (राजयोग), and public deeds.
- **10th Lord Placements:**
  - In Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10): Prominent career visibility and steady advancement.
  - In Trikona (1, 5, 9): Ethical career, advisory roles, educational or creative vocation.
  - In 2nd or 11th: Highly profitable profession, wealth generation through personal trade.

## 2. The 6th House (सेवा भाव - Job, Daily Work & Competition)
- **Significance:** Salaried employment (नौकरी), service to others, clearing competitive exams, dealing with coworkers, and handling professional deadlines.
- Benefics or strong Upachaya rulers in the 6th house bestow the resilience needed to succeed in competitive corporate or civil service examinations.

## 3. Planetary Significators of Career (आजीविका के कारक ग्रह)
- **Surya (Sun / सूर्य):** Government administration, high-level civil authority, medicine, leadership, politics, public enterprise.
- **Mangala (Mars / मंगल):** Engineering, technology, defense, police, surgery, real estate development, entrepreneurship, technical operations. When exalted in 10th house (Makara / Capricorn), forms the prestigious **Ruchaka Mahapurusha Yoga**, bestowing executive command.
- **Budha (Mercury / बुध):** Information technology, software coding, data analysis, accounting, finance, journalism, writing, commerce, trading.
- **Guru (Jupiter / बृहस्पति):** Teaching, law, banking, management consulting, judiciary, spiritual instruction, advisory services.
- **Shukra (Venus / शुक्र):** Arts, design, hospitality, entertainment, luxury brands, media, architecture, diplomacy.
- **Shani (Saturn / शनि):** Law, heavy industry, manufacturing, labor management, governance, research, mining. A strong Saturn gives enduring career longevity and steady climb through disciplined work.
- **Rahu (राहु):** Artificial intelligence, cutting-edge technology, foreign corporations (MNCs), aviation, unconventional innovations.

## 4. Job Timing and Promotions (दशा एवं गोचर)
- Favorable career breakthroughs and promotions typically materialize during the Mahadasha or Antardasha of planets occupying or aspecting the 10th house, the 10th lord, or the Lagna lord.
- Career shifts often coincide with Saturn or Jupiter transits over the natal 10th house or natal Sun.

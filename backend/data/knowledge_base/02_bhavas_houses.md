# The Twelve Bhavas: Houses of Life in Vedic Astrology

In Vedic astrology, the sky is partitioned into twelve Bhavas (houses) anchored by the Lagna (Ascendant), representing the intersection of the eastern horizon and the ecliptic at the exact time and place of birth.

## The Four Purusharthas (Life Pursuits)
- **Dharma Houses (Righteous Purpose & Duty):** 1st, 5th, and 9th Houses.
- **Artha Houses (Wealth, Material Stability & Resources):** 2nd, 6th, and 10th Houses.
- **Kama Houses (Desire, Relationships & Ambition):** 3rd, 7th, and 11th Houses.
- **Moksha Houses (Liberation, Spirituality & Inner Solitude):** 4th, 8th, and 12th Houses.

## House Classifications
- **Kendra Houses (Pillars of Chart):** 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th. Strongest operational arenas of life.
- **Trikona Houses (Trines of Grace):** 1st, 5th, and 9th. Naturally auspicious and carry supportive karma.
- **Upachaya Houses (Growth Over Time):** 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 11th. Challenges in these houses improve with age and discipline.
- **Dusthana Houses (Transformation & Obstacle):** 6th, 8th, and 12th. Govern hurdles, debts, transformations, and detachment.

## Detailed Bhava Significations

### 1st House (Tanu Bhava - Self & Vitality)
- Self, physical constitution, vitality, basic worldview, personal appearance, head, personality essence.

### 2nd House (Dhana Bhava - Wealth & Lineage)
- Accumulated wealth, liquid assets, speech, family lineage, food habits, face, early childhood upbringing.

### 3rd House (Sahaja Bhava - Courage & Communication)
- Courage, younger siblings, short journeys, manual skills, writing, communication channels, initiative.

### 4th House (Sukha Bhava - Home & Happiness)
- Mother, inner happiness, emotional peace, home, real estate, vehicles, foundational education, heart.

### 5th House (Putra Bhava - Intellect & Creativity)
- Children, intellect, creative intelligence, speculative ventures, Purva Punya (past meritorious actions), mantras.

### 6th House (Ari Bhava - Service & Obstacles)
- Daily work, service, enemies, litigation, health vulnerabilities, debts, discipline through routines, digestive system.

### 7th House (Yuvati Bhava - Partnership & Marriage)
- Marriage, long-term romantic relationships, business partners, public dealings, foreign travel.

### 8th House (Randhra Bhava - Longevity & Transformation)
- Longevity, sudden changes, esoteric knowledge, inheritance, research, psychological depth, secrets.

### 9th House (Dharma Bhava - Higher Wisdom & Fortune)
- Father, spiritual guru, higher learning, pilgrimages, moral philosophy, luck, fortune, ethics.

### 10th House (Karma Bhava - Profession & Status)
- Career, public reputation, social authority, professional accomplishments, status in society, government.

### 11th House (Labha Bhava - Gains & Aspirations)
- Financial gains, elder siblings, social networks, groups, fulfillment of long-term desires, friendship circles.

### 12th House (Vyaya Bhava - Solitude & Release)
- Expenses, foreign residence, meditation, spiritual release, sleep quality, subconscious mind, charity.
